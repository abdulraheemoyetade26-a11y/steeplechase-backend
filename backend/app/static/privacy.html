Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Thaw. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

1. What we collect

We collect your email address, password (hashed and secured), the lecture files you upload, the images extracted from them, and your practice scores.

2. How we use your data

Your lecture files are processed using AI (Google Gemini) to extract labels for practice. We do not sell your data. We do not share your files with other students.

3. Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings page. When you do, all your files, scores, and personal data are permanently deleted from our database.
